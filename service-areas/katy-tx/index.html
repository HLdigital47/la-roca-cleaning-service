Practical questions before cleaning in Katy

What if my movers change the date after I request cleaning?

Tell La Roca as soon as you know and explain when the home will actually be empty. Ask to confirm new availability before changing the key handover. If only part of the property will be clear, identify those areas to discuss a different scope.

The seller still occupies the home. Can I arrange cleaning before closing?

Obtain access permission from the person responsible for the property and confirm when belongings will be removed. A closing date does not always mean the home will be empty that day. Share the actual access date with La Roca before scheduling the visit.

How do I ask the team to avoid a room or leave certain objects untouched?

Identify excluded rooms and objects that must stay in place before the visit. Put delicate items and documents away and leave a contact available for questions. The agreed checklist should distinguish included areas from restricted ones.

What if a mark remains visible even after the surface is clean?

Identify the location, material and how long the mark has been there. A mark can be wear, discoloration or finish damage that needs a different assessment. Share a photo before trying another product or asking for stronger treatment.

Can I coordinate cleaning for a Katy property while living in another city?

Designate an authorized access contact and share tasks and property rules ahead of time. Agree on who can answer questions during the visit and how the work will be reviewed. Service confirmation should cover availability and access even when you are not present.
