Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning in Stafford

Should I request house cleaning or commercial cleaning for my Stafford property?

Choose based on how the space is used. A home needs a household checklist; a workplace needs its occupied areas, restroom use, customer traffic and access rules considered. For a home office, explain whether you want the whole home cleaned or only a business workspace.

What should a Stafford office estimate include besides square footage?

List restrooms, kitchen or break room, floor types, bins, desks and cleaning frequency. Note which areas customers use and which rooms are restricted. Two offices with the same floor area can require very different work if one has more occupants or heavily used restrooms.

Can I request cleaning after my business closes?

Provide the building's permitted access hours, alarm procedure and the time the work must finish. After-hours requests depend on the route and available appointment. Agree on how the team enters, which areas are accessible and who locks up before confirming a visit.

Does general commercial cleaning include machinery, warehouse waste or floor stripping?

Those jobs need a separate scope review. Routine office cleaning is not the same as servicing machinery, removing industrial waste or stripping and refinishing floors. Identify the material and work area first; do not assume that a general cleaning estimate covers specialist work.
