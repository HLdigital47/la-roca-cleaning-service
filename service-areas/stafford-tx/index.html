Questions from people coordinating a Stafford property

Can I send quote photos without showing people or private information?

Yes, you can share images of the areas needing cleaning. Keep documents, screens, personal photographs and entry codes out of the shot. An overall view and a close-up of the buildup often explain the work better than a photo filled with unrelated belongings.

The tenant still has belongings in the property. Are they considered trash?

Do not label belongings as waste simply because the home is being handed over. Define with the property contact what stays, who removes it and which surfaces will be accessible. Communicate those decisions before cleaning to avoid unclear instructions.

We moved furniture and found dirt outside the original scope. What should we do?

Share the location and a photo with La Roca, and explain whether the furniture will remain out of the area. Ask to review the task rather than assuming it can be added at the same price or within the same visit time. The surface must be accessible to assess the work.

I have two suites in the same building. Can I include them in one request?

You can explain both during follow-up, but identify each unit, its size, use, bathrooms and access. Even in the same building, tasks and hours can differ. Ask for a scope that makes clear what applies to each suite before confirming.

What if some people consider boxes or papers trash while others need them?

Ask the property contact to identify them before the visit. Separate retained items from authorized waste and note any building recycling procedure. Decisions about documents and belongings should be resolved by the person responsible for the space.
