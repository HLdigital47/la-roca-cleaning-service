Apartment and move-out cleaning in Westchase

Is cleaning my occupied Westchase apartment the same as a move-out clean?

No. In an occupied apartment, belongings limit access and the focus is upkeep. An empty-home clean can include the agreed cabinet, drawer and appliance interiors. State whether furniture and food will be removed, then match the work to the actual handover instructions.

Should I turn off utilities before my apartment cleaning?

Arrange the cleaning while water, power and safe access are still available. Set the shutoff date after the work is finished, subject to your lease and utility arrangements. Returning keys or ending access too early can prevent the scheduled cleaning from taking place.

Does move-out cleaning include carpet shampooing?

Vacuuming and carpet extraction are different jobs. Check whether your apartment requires a separate carpet service or receipt, and raise that requirement before booking. Do not assume a general move-out cleaning price includes stain treatment, deodorizing or a specialist carpet-cleaning appointment.

What access details matter for a Westchase apartment appointment?

Provide the unit and building, gate or office check-in procedure, permitted parking and any stair or elevator restrictions during follow-up. Confirm who will let the team in. Include the key-return deadline so the visit can be planned around a real access window.
