Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning in Missouri City

Do I have to book recurring cleaning if I only need help once?

A one-time visit and ongoing maintenance are different requests. Describe whether this is preparation for guests, a first reset or an empty-home move. La Roca can review the appropriate service and appointment without treating a one-time request as a recurring schedule.

My home has unused bedrooms. Should I still include them in the estimate?

List the rooms you want cleaned and the rooms to leave closed. Include shared bathrooms, hallways and floor areas that still need attention. This lets the scope be reviewed accurately; an unused bedroom does not automatically mean the whole home requires less work.

How should I arrange access if I'm away from my Missouri City home?

Agree directly on entry, alarm instructions and who can answer questions during the visit. Confirm any gated-community requirements and a way to secure the property afterward. Send sensitive access details privately, rather than putting them in public comments or shared cleaning lists.

What needs extra planning when there are pets in the house?

Mention the animals, shedding areas and any rooms they use heavily. Arrange a secure, comfortable place for them while doors are being opened and products are in use. Distinguish ordinary pet hair from stains, odor treatment or upholstery cleaning when discussing priorities.
