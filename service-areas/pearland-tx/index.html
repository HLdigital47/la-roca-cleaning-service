Situations to discuss before your visit

I work from home. How can I avoid interruptions during a video call?

Identify the room you need to use and the hours involved. Agree with La Roca whether other areas can be cleaned first and when your workspace will be available. Point out equipment and documents that must not be touched; confirm any scheduling change rather than assuming it is included.

I am sensitive to certain fragrances. What should I mention before booking?

Share the fragrances or products you need to avoid before scheduling. Ask La Roca to review possible alternatives and whether they suit your surfaces. Do not assume a fragrance-free product is suitable for every allergy or material.

What if there is no water or electricity on cleaning day?

Tell La Roca as soon as you know. Water and electricity may be needed for the agreed tasks. Discuss whether to reschedule or whether a practical scope is possible before keeping the appointment.

I want to add a bathroom or refrigerator interior after receiving my estimate. How do I request it?

Communicate the additional tasks before the visit, including their condition and whether they will be accessible. Ask to confirm any price or time adjustment so the team receives the same service checklist you expect.

My pet tries to escape when the door opens. How should I prepare?

Arrange a safe space separate from the entrance and areas being cleaned. Explain which doors or gates must stay closed and leave a contact available. Agree on access before the visit so the instructions are clear.
