Planning detailed cleaning in Memorial

Can deep cleaning fix cloudy marble or dull wood floors?

First distinguish residue from damage to the finish. Dirt or product film may be removable; etching, worn coatings and scratches need a different assessment. Share the surface material and any care instructions before cleaning, especially for stone and older wood finishes.

What about high ceiling fans, chandeliers and tall windows?

Identify their height and how they can be reached. A house-cleaning quote should distinguish safely reachable surfaces from work requiring specialist equipment or elevated access. Send a description or photo during follow-up instead of assuming every high fixture is included.

Should I book a whole-house deep clean after a kitchen remodel?

Describe the renovation first. Dust may have spread beyond the kitchen, so list adjoining rooms, ledges, cabinet interiors and floors that need review. Final cleaning works best once trades are finished and new finishes are ready for their manufacturer's recommended care.

Can I prioritize used rooms without cleaning every room in my Memorial home?

Give La Roca a room-by-room list for review, including guest rooms to leave closed, stairs, bathrooms and common areas. Mark delicate objects or display shelves that should not be handled. Defining those boundaries is more useful than estimating the job from bedroom count alone.
