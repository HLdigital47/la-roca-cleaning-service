Questions that come up after requesting move-out cleaning

I was quoted an hourly rate. Is it for the crew or for each person?

Ask before accepting: how many people, which rate applies and whether time is charged per crew or per person. Clarify how any change in crew size is approved. Review the pricing basis of your La Roca quote before confirming the visit.

I paid for professional cleaning, but management wants to charge for another clean. What information do I need?

Ask which areas were flagged and request the relevant photos or report. Gather your quote, receipt and handover photos to distinguish the agreed work from another task or a later condition. Share any service-related observation with La Roca; a receipt alone does not explain what was found in the home.

What if the home needs more work on arrival than the photos showed?

Ask for a clear explanation of the difference and review any change to work, time or price before approving it. Keep an authorized contact available during the visit. If the scope changes, record the agreement instead of waiting until the invoice to clarify it.

My booked cleaning service is running late and I return keys tomorrow. Can I ask for help?

Call or message La Roca with the exact deadline, whether the home is empty and how entry can be arranged. Availability must be reviewed before a visit is confirmed. If time is too limited, ask the person receiving the home what access alternatives they can authorize; do not assume an appointment exists until it is confirmed.

The carpet has already been professionally washed. What should I clarify before another visit?

Share the specialist’s drying instructions and identify which areas can be walked through. Coordinate any providers still due to enter and mention rooms that cannot yet be accessed. If marks appear, document where they are and when you noticed them so the situation can be reviewed without assuming their source.
