What does move-in or move-out cleaning cover?

An empty property allows access to the areas that furniture and belongings usually cover. We use your move-in priorities or the property’s handover checklist to agree on the cleaning tasks.

Kitchen

  1. Counters, sink and cabinet fronts
  2. Accessible kitchen surfaces
  3. Floors after belongings are removed

Bathrooms

  1. Sinks, mirrors and fixtures
  2. Toilets, showers and bathtubs
  3. Floors and accessible surfaces

Rooms and floors

  1. Accessible surfaces in empty rooms
  2. Floors exposed after moving furniture out
  3. The rooms and shared areas included in the estimate

Empty storage interiors

  1. Selected cabinet and drawer interiors
  2. Selected closet shelves
  3. Access to cleared interiors before unpacking or handover

Frequently Asked Questions About Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

What is different about move-in and move-out cleaning?

Both need an empty-home scope, but the priorities differ. Move-out work follows the agreed handover requirements; move-in work prepares areas you will use before unpacking. Specify cabinets, drawers, appliances and closets instead of assuming either service includes every interior.

Can the cleaning happen while movers or contractors are still there?

It is better to finish moving and dusty repair work first. Otherwise, cleaned floors and surfaces may be dirtied again and access may remain blocked. Coordinate a cleaning window after those jobs and before the key handover or furniture delivery.

What should I do if my lease requires professional carpet cleaning?

Treat that as a separate requirement to verify with the property manager. Vacuuming during a general clean is not carpet extraction. Ask what service and documentation the property requires, then confirm who will perform it and how its timing fits the rest of the move.

Does hiring a move-out cleaner mean the property is ready for every inspection?

Use the property's actual checklist to agree on the cleaning. Repairs, paint, damaged surfaces and other lease matters are separate from removing dirt. Review the finished rooms and retain photographs and any service documentation before returning possession.
