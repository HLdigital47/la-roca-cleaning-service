Questions when arranging recurring home visits

What if I need to skip a visit while on vacation?

Contact La Roca when your plans change and review your booking terms. Confirm the next date directly. If the gap between visits grows, review the home’s condition and the scope for the next cleaning.

Can I change which rooms take priority from one visit to the next?

Communicate changes before the visit and identify the rooms you want prioritized. Review whether the new tasks fit the agreed scope or require an adjustment. Prioritizing a room does not mean every additional task is included.

I work from home. How do we coordinate calls and meetings?

Share times when you need quiet and identify the occupied room. Agree which areas can be cleaned first and when your workspace becomes available. Confirm with the team whether the proposed order is workable.

My pet gets nervous around visitors. What should I prepare?

Mention pets before the appointment and arrange a safe space with a responsible person where needed. Share instructions to prevent pets escaping through open doors. Clearly identify any area that must stay closed.

What should I do if I notice a missed detail after a visit?

Send a photo of the specific area, the service date and when you noticed it. Explain whether the room was used afterward. This helps review the detail against agreed tasks and determine the next step.

Can we start with a few rooms and add more later?

Identify the rooms you want included initially and those left out. If you later want to expand, communicate the additional areas so tasks, access and pricing can be reviewed before the next visit.
