Frequently Asked Questions About Recurring House Cleaning

Should I choose weekly, every two weeks or once a month?

Base the interval on what builds up between visits, not just the size of the house. Busy kitchens, frequently used bathrooms and shedding pets may need more upkeep. A monthly visit may work when you maintain those areas yourself. Review the expected tasks at each interval.

Does an initial deep clean have to be included in a recurring plan?

The current condition determines whether an initial reset is useful. Identify accumulated grease, bathroom residue and neglected details before setting the maintenance checklist. Ask for the first-visit scope and ongoing scope separately rather than assuming the same work or price applies to both.

Do oven interiors and baseboards get cleaned at every recurring visit?

Only if they are part of the agreed checklist. Some detail tasks may be scheduled periodically or quoted separately instead of being repeated each time. Keep a list of those tasks and their frequency so the ongoing service matches what you expect.

What should I do before skipping or moving a scheduled visit?

Contact La Roca as soon as your plans change and review the applicable booking terms. A longer gap may change the condition of the home or the work needed next time. Confirm the new date and any scope changes directly instead of assuming the original appointment simply shifts.
