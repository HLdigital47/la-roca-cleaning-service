What gets cleaned inside your apartment

Use these areas as a starting point for agreeing scope. Initial condition and available surfaces determine the detail needed.

Kitchen

Attention to the space where you prepare and share meals.

  • Accessible counters and backsplash.
  • Sink, faucet and stovetop care suited to the material.
  • Cabinet and appliance exteriors.
  • Floors; separately confirm any interiors needed.

Bathrooms

Care for everyday surfaces, considering materials and buildup.

  • Toilets, sinks and faucets.
  • Mirrors and countertops.
  • Shower or tub within the agreed scope.
  • Floors and accessible cabinet exteriors.

Bedrooms

Cleaning accessible areas around your belongings.

  • Dusting nightstands, dressers and clear surfaces.
  • Mirrors and accessible furniture suited to the finish.
  • Vacuuming or mopping available floor areas.
  • Identify fragile objects and rooms you want left out.

Living, dining and floors

Attention to areas for relaxing, welcoming visitors and moving through your home.

  • Tables and accessible surfaces.
  • Dusting agreed furniture and shelves.
  • Vacuuming or mopping suited to the floor type.
  • Touchpoints and waste bins on the agreed task list.

Visit frequency based on your routine, not just square footage

Ask about weekly, every-two-week or monthly cleaning. Kitchen use, bathroom count, pets and upkeep between visits help determine the interval. If dirt has already built up, consider a deep cleaning assessment first.

How to prepare your apartment for cleaning

Questions before welcoming the cleaning team

I will not be home. How do I authorize entry?

Agree in advance on an access method allowed by the building and leave a reachable contact. Check whether the front desk or management needs resident authorization. Confirm entry and lock-up arrangements with La Roca before the visit.

I share an apartment. Can we book only the common areas?

Identify spaces to include and bedrooms that should remain outside the service. Make sure everyone knows about the visit and leaves agreed areas available. The quote should reflect that scope rather than assuming the entire unit will be cleaned.

The building changed service-provider hours. What should I do?

Tell La Roca as soon as you learn about the restriction. Share permitted hours and ask to review availability before assuming a schedule change is confirmed. Review elevator or access bookings too, if applicable.

My landlord requires a handover checklist. Can I use it to request cleaning?

Share it before requesting a quote to distinguish cleaning tasks from maintenance or other service requirements. Confirm the agreed cleaning scope in writing. Management is responsible for accepting the unit and deciding on the deposit.

My apartment was just treated for pests. When can I arrange cleaning?

Ask the pest-control company for reentry and cleaning instructions, including surfaces that should remain untouched. Share these with La Roca before scheduling. Timing depends on the treatment used rather than a general waiting period.

I noticed a missed detail after the visit. How can we review it?

Send a clear photo of the area, the service date and when you noticed the detail. Explain whether the space was used after cleaning. This helps review the issue against agreed tasks and determine the next step.
