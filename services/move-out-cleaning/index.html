Frequently Asked Questions About Move-Out Cleaning

Which areas should I check before requesting move-out cleaning?

Start with the landlord's or buyer's list. Check oven and fridge interiors, cabinet shelves, drawers, bathrooms, floor edges and leftover trash. Identify any repairs or separate carpet requirement. Send those priorities during follow-up so the cleaning is based on your actual handover needs.

Can I book the cleaning for the same time as the final inspection?

Leave a buffer between completion and inspection so you can review the work and address any remaining items. Moving delays, blocked rooms or unavailable utilities can affect the visit. Provide the key-return deadline when requesting the appointment, not after the work has started.

Will cleaning deal with wall damage, carpet stains and lingering pet odors?

These need separate assessment. Removing ordinary dirt is different from repainting, repairing surfaces, extracting carpet stains or treating persistent odors. Identify each issue before booking so the estimate does not confuse a cleaning task with a repair or specialist service.

What records should I keep after the cleaning?

Keep the agreed scope, any service invoice or receipt, and clear photos of the finished rooms and appliance interiors. Review the property's checklist before returning the keys. Cleaning documentation records work performed; it does not settle separate questions about damage or other tenancy charges.
