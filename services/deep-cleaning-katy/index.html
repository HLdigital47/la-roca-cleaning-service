Frequently Asked Questions About Deep Cleaning in Katy

I bought a Katy home with furniture still inside. Can it be deep-cleaned before I move in?

First identify when the belongings will leave. An occupied-home deep clean cannot reach the same areas as an empty-home visit. Plan cabinet, closet and appliance interiors around the actual access date, and explain whether you need a deep clean now or move-in cleaning after removal.

Can I prioritize the kitchen and bathrooms before upstairs bedrooms?

List those areas in priority order and include the bathroom count, stairs and any rooms to exclude. Have La Roca review that scope before booking. Prioritizing helps allocate the agreed work; it does not automatically add more tasks to a fixed appointment.

Will a deep clean include washing walls or every window?

Do not assume it does. Identify marks, paint finish, glass surfaces, height and access before the estimate. Spot cleaning and whole-wall washing are different tasks, as are reachable interior glass and elevated or exterior windows. Each needs a clear scope review.

What details help price a deep clean in Katy accurately?

Have the area to clean, bathrooms, floor levels and recent cleaning history ready. Describe grease, shower residue and appliance interiors separately. During follow-up, photos of the priority areas can make the condition clearer than saying the house needs a really deep clean.
