Airbnb cleaning in Houston: host questions

What does La Roca’s Airbnb cleaning include?

Cleaning of the agreed kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, plus sheet changes, laundering of bedding and towels, and photo reporting of damage noticed during the visit. We review the property layout, beds, laundry arrangements and presentation with you before scheduling.

Can you clean between checkout and a same-day check-in?

Share both times, the bed and bathroom count, and whether laundry will be done on site. La Roca reviews the available window before confirming. A clean spare set for each bed helps prepare rooms while the used set is being washed and dried.

Do you change sheets and wash towels?

Yes. La Roca offers sheet changes and washing and drying of bedding and towels. Tell us how many beds and towel sets the property uses, where clean sets are stored and how laundry access will work so the visit can be planned around the next arrival.

How will I find out if you notice damage?

La Roca reports damage noticed during cleaning with photographs. Before the first visit, agree on the contact who should receive the report. Include photos of any existing marks in your property instructions so the team has a reference.

What should I send before the first cleaning?

Start with the property address, checkout and check-in times, bed and bathroom counts, entry instructions and laundry arrangements. Photos showing your preferred bed and towel setup help explain how you want the property presented.

When does a rental need deep cleaning instead of a turnover?

A turnover focuses on cleaning and preparing the accommodation between stays. Review a separate deep clean when grease, bathroom residue or dust in less frequently reached areas needs more time than the normal visit. Identify those areas when planning a gap between bookings.
