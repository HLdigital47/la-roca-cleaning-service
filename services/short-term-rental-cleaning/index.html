Turnover questions for short-term rental hosts

Is a rental turnover the same as a regular house clean?

A turnover also needs the property reset for the next arrival: agreed bedding, towels, supplies and presentation, alongside cleaning. Define who handles each task and use a property-specific checklist. A normal occupied-home visit does not automatically include that guest-readiness work.

Can a turnover fit between a morning checkout and an afternoon check-in?

It depends on the property, its condition, access and laundry plan. Share the actual checkout and check-in times before confirming a visit. Allow room for late departures or extra cleaning; an available calendar slot alone does not establish that all turnover tasks fit.

Who should wash the sheets and towels?

Assign washing, drying, replacement and storage explicitly. On-site laundry can take longer than the cleaning window, particularly with multiple loads. Having clean spare sets available reduces dependence on a single wash cycle. Confirm whether any laundry work is part of La Roca's agreed scope.

What should my turnover checklist say about supplies and damage?

State where owner-provided supplies are kept, target quantities and who is responsible for replenishing them. Separate cleaning tasks from documenting missing or damaged items. Agree on how concerns should be reported and who decides the next step before another guest arrives.
