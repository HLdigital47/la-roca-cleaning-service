Frequently Asked Questions About House Cleaning

What should a regular house-cleaning checklist actually say?

It should name the rooms and tasks, such as accessible dusting, floors, kitchen surfaces and bathroom fixtures. It should also state how appliance interiors and detailed work are handled. A phrase such as complete cleaning is not enough to compare two estimates accurately.

Do I need to clean the house before the cleaner comes?

You do not need to scrub first. Pick up clothes, toys and loose belongings so surfaces and floors can be reached. Set aside valuables and identify rooms or items not to touch. Cleaning dirt and sorting possessions are different tasks, so agree on any organizing work separately.

Why might my first cleaning need more work than later visits?

The first visit may uncover grease, dust or bathroom buildup that has accumulated over time. Later maintenance visits start from a different condition. Describe the current state honestly and ask what belongs in the initial visit versus the recurring checklist before comparing the two prices.

Can I be home while the cleaning is done?

Discuss the room order and any work calls, children or pets that affect access. Keep the areas being cleaned available and identify a person who can answer questions. If you prefer to be away, agree directly on entry and how the home will be secured afterward.
