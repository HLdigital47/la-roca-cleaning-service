Frequently Asked Questions About Commercial and Office Cleaning

What information is needed for a useful commercial cleaning quote?

Provide the area to clean, business use, occupancy, restroom count, floor types and proposed frequency. Identify customer areas and restricted rooms. A floor plan or walkthrough can clarify the work; square footage alone does not capture restroom use, traffic or the level of detail needed.

How do we decide which tasks happen every visit and which are periodic?

Separate routine needs such as bins, floors and restrooms from periodic details such as interior appliances or high ledges. Agree on a written schedule by area and task. This makes the price and the expected work easier to review than a general promise to clean everything.

Can cleaning take place without staff being present?

Agree on the permitted time, authorized entry method, alarm steps and lock-up responsibility. Keep a reachable contact for questions and mark restricted areas. An after-hours request needs confirmed availability and building permission before a visit can be scheduled.

Are paper products, soap, carpet extraction and floor finishing included?

Define each item separately. Cleaning labor, replenishing client-provided supplies, buying consumables and specialist floor work are different responsibilities. Check what the estimate covers and who provides supplies; a general office-cleaning quote should not be assumed to include all four.
