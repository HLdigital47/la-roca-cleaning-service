Frequently Asked Questions About Move-Out Cleaning in Pearland

I'm selling my Pearland home. Is the cleaning scope the same as a rental move-out?

Both can need empty-home cleaning, but the handover instructions may differ. Use the buyer's or property's agreed requirements to identify appliances staying behind, cabinet interiors and rooms included. Cleaning should be scheduled after moving work and before the relevant walkthrough.

Does cleaning the kitchen mean the fridge and oven are cleaned inside too?

Not automatically. List each interior explicitly and explain whether it will be empty. Grease, spills and remaining food affect the work. The estimate should distinguish wiping the appliance exterior from cleaning shelves, drawers or the oven cavity.

What should I do about pet stains before the final walkthrough?

Identify the affected surface and ask whether it needs carpet extraction, odor treatment, repair or ordinary cleaning. Those are different jobs. Share the property's requirements before booking and arrange any specialist service separately rather than expecting a general clean to resolve every pet-related mark.

What should I review before giving up access to the Pearland property?

Walk through the rooms against the agreed cleaning list, checking selected appliance and cabinet interiors as well as visible surfaces. Take photos while the property is still accessible and retain service documentation. Return keys after your review and any other scheduled work is complete.
