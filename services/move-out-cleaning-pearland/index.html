THE ORDER MATTERS

Fewer loose ends. More clarity before handover.

An empty home may still have work left before cleaning can begin. Identify who will enter after the movers, what remains unresolved and when you will make your final visit.

  1. 01

    Empty the home

    Confirm when boxes, furniture and belongings that will not stay are leaving. If anything remains, identify the areas that will still be occupied.

  2. 02

    Resolve pending work

    Place repairs, painting and dust-producing work before cleaning. Confirm who removes materials when each job is finished.

  3. 03

    Clean and review

    Coordinate cleaning with water, electricity and authorized access available. Leave time to walk through the home before returning keys.

Different handovers need different plans.

Choose the situation that matches your move. Share useful context from the first conversation instead of requesting a generic visit.

Returning a rental

Ask your management team for current move-out instructions. Share any pre-inspection observations and distinguish cleaning requests from repair requirements.

Selling a house

Align the visit with belongings leaving and the final walkthrough arranged with your agent or buyer. Clarify which appliances and furnishings will remain.

Preparing for a new occupant

Owners and managers can coordinate access after the actual move-out. Share outstanding maintenance and the next occupancy date so availability can be reviewed.

A mark is not always a cleaning problem.

Identify what you are seeing before booking more work. One close-up and one wider photograph help explain the concern; an in-person assessment may still be needed.

Dirt or buildup

Describe how long it has been there, the material and any products already used. Avoid trying new products before checking compatibility with the finish.

Wear or damage

A scratch, peeling paint or a deteriorated finish may require repair. Do not treat it as a stain that cleaning will necessarily remove.

A persistent odor

Mention pets, moisture or smoke. Understanding the source helps identify whether another specialist is needed; fragrance does not address the cause.

Questions that come up before a Pearland handover

My roommate is staying. Can I book move-out cleaning?

You can ask about cleaning the areas you will leave empty, but explain that the home will remain occupied. Agree on accessible spaces and who authorizes entry. Separately ask management how it handles one occupant moving out; cleaning does not change that arrangement.

What if the movers are not finished before the cleaning appointment?

Let La Roca know as soon as the expected departure changes. Review whether access and available areas allow the plan to proceed or whether the visit needs to change, subject to availability. Do not assume the same work can be completed among boxes and furniture.

I cleaned myself, but management noted a few issues. Can I send that list?

Yes. Share the observations exactly as received, with photos and the time you still have access. La Roca can review the request and quote the agreed work; you do not need to describe the whole home as though you were starting from scratch.

Should I leave the refrigerator turned off and closed before handover?

Check manufacturer and property instructions before disconnecting it. Remove food and explain its condition when requesting cleaning. Agree with the person receiving the property on how it should be left operating; do not change settings simply to prepare for the visit.

Carpet cleaning is booked separately. How should I coordinate the visits?

Confirm access, clear areas and the specialist’s drying instructions with both providers. Avoid placing movers or dust-producing work afterward. If the property asks for a specific receipt, verify that it matches the service requested.

Do I need to be present to discuss an observation at the end?

Agree beforehand on a contact and how the agreed areas can be reviewed. If you will be away from Pearland, arrange authorized access and someone who can help with a walkthrough. Confirm communication arrangements with La Roca before scheduling.
