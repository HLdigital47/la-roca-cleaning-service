Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning in Houston

Why did I get two very different prices for the same house?

Compare the actual work, not just the number of bedrooms. Check bathrooms, occupied square footage, buildup, visit frequency and appliance interiors. A maintenance visit and a first deep clean are different jobs, even at the same address.

Do I need a regular clean, a deep clean or a move-out clean?

Choose regular cleaning for ongoing upkeep. Consider a deep clean for accumulated grease, bathroom buildup and neglected details. For a change of occupants, request move-in or move-out cleaning and identify empty cabinets, appliances and any handover requirements.

Does booking cleaning include laundry, organizing and cleaning inside the fridge?

These are separate tasks and should be listed explicitly. Wiping an appliance exterior is different from emptying and cleaning its interior. Likewise, cleaning a bedroom does not automatically include sorting clothes or washing bedding. Agree on these details before the visit.

What do I need to fill out to request an estimate?

The estimate form asks only for your name, cell phone number and service. During follow-up, have your approximate square footage, bathroom count and priorities ready. For a move, also have the handover date and the landlord's cleaning list available.
