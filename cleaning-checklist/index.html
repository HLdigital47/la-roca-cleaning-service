Frequently Asked Questions About the Cleaning Checklist

Does checking a task mean it is automatically included in my cleaning?

No. The checklist helps you identify priorities; the final scope still needs review. Point out appliance interiors, height restrictions, heavy buildup and specialist needs. A checked item is a request to discuss, not confirmation of price, availability or inclusion.

How should I choose priorities if I cannot do everything at once?

Start with the rooms used most and the specific dirt that bothers you: kitchen grease, bathroom residue or floor buildup. Then add lower-priority details. Separate necessary cleaning from organizing and repairs so a time-limited visit can focus on a realistic set of tasks.

Should I use the same list for an occupied home and an empty rental?

Use different scopes. An occupied-home list must consider furniture and belongings; an empty-rental list should reflect the property's actual handover instructions. Cabinet and appliance interiors become more accessible after moving, but still need to be specifically included in the agreed work.

What is the best way to discuss my selected tasks with La Roca?

Keep your list available for the follow-up call or send it privately on WhatsApp. Name your highest priorities first and identify unusual surfaces or access limits. The estimate form remains simple: name, cell phone number and service; additional details can be discussed afterward.
