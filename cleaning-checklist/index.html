Frequently Asked Questions About the Cleaning Checklist
Does selecting tasks confirm a booking?
No. La Roca must confirm the scope, price and date with you before scheduling service.
Can I change my checklist after sending it?
Yes. Update your selections and send the revised summary. Tell La Roca it replaces your previous list to avoid confusion.
What if I use a shared device?
Use “Clear my selection” when finished. Your selection stays in that browser and another person using it could see it.
Does my PDF change when I edit my tasks?
The downloaded file keeps the selection from when it was generated. If you change tasks or notes, download a new version before sharing it.
