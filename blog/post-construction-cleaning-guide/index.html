Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning After Construction

How do I know the site is ready for final cleaning?

Confirm that dusty work is complete, tools and major debris are removed, access is safe and water and power are available. Check care instructions and curing requirements for new finishes with the contractor. Final cleaning should not be scheduled on the assumption that unfinished work will be done in time.

What should I photograph when requesting a post-construction estimate?

Show the rooms affected, dust on ledges and floors, cabinet interiors and specific residue on identifiable surfaces. Include an overall view as well as close-ups. Explain the work performed and access limits; a close-up alone may not show how widespread the cleanup is.

Who should assess paint overspray, adhesive or grout haze?

Identify the material and residue with the contractor or installer before removal is attempted. Different finishes can react differently to scrapers and chemicals. Have specialist removal needs separated from ordinary dust cleaning instead of assuming every residue can be removed with the same method.

Should I plan another review after the final punch-list work?

Yes, if trades return after cleaning, inspect the areas they use or disturb. New dust and footprints can be introduced by even a small repair. Identify any follow-up cleaning required and review it as new work instead of assuming it belongs to the earlier final-clean appointment.
