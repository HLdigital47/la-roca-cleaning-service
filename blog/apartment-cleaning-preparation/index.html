Arrange access before arrival

Share building entry instructions, parking rules and elevator restrictions if relevant. Put away valuables, fragile items and personal paperwork. Inform the team about pets and rooms that should not be entered.

Set a practical priority list

For a limited visit, prioritize the kitchen, bathroom and frequently used floors. Pick up belongings that block surfaces. For a full apartment service, describe the room count, current condition and additional tasks before booking.
