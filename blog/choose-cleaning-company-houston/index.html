Compare like-for-like estimates

Ask what rooms and tasks are included, how extra work is priced and whether the estimate covers the entire visit or individual cleaner hours. Compare the same scope between providers. Check recent customer feedback and ask for relevant credentials when needed.

Confirm the appointment details

Before accepting, confirm the final price, access instructions, cancellation arrangements and any restrictions. Communicate allergies, delicate surfaces and pets. A clear written scope helps both customer and cleaning team understand expectations.
