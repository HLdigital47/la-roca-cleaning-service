Frequently Asked Questions About Choosing a Cleaning Company in Houston

What should I ask before comparing two companies' prices?

Ask both to describe rooms, tasks, extras, frequency and initial condition using the same information. Confirm whether each proposal is a checklist-based service or a time block. You can then compare what you are buying instead of comparing two totals for different jobs.

How can I check reviews without treating a star rating as the whole story?

Read the service type, recency and details of the customer's experience, and see how the business responds. A review about maintenance may not tell you much about a construction cleanup. Use reviews alongside a clear scope discussion and direct answers to your own questions.

What should I ask about access, insurance and accidental damage?

Ask who enters the property, how access instructions are handled and what process applies if a concern occurs. If insurance matters to you or the building, request current documentation directly. Do not infer coverage or procedures from the word professional or from another company's policies.

Which terms should be clear before I confirm a cleaning appointment?

Review the agreed work, appointment window, price, extras, payment method and change or cancellation terms. Ask how concerns about the completed work are handled. Keep the confirmed details so both sides can review the same arrangement instead of relying on assumptions.
